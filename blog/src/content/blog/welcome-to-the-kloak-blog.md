This is the first post on the Kloak blog. We will use this space to share product announcements, post-release deep dives, and security insights from the team building Kloak.

## What you can expect

We plan to publish a few different kinds of posts here:

- **Release notes** — when we ship a new version, we'll explain what changed and why.
- **Technical deep dives** — how Kloak intercepts TLS writes, the eBPF tricks we use, and the trade-offs we made along the way.
- **Security disclosures** — vulnerabilities we find or fix, with the context you need to evaluate impact.
- **Field notes** — patterns we see in real deployments, and lessons we learn from running Kloak in production environments.

## Why a blog and not just docs?

Documentation explains *what something does*. A blog explains *why it exists, how it changed, and what we learned getting there*. Both have a place. The [docs](/docs/) cover the reference material; this is where you'll find the story behind it.
